sprites.png: the Sprites example's pixel art, drawn for this repo entirely from code - a Node
script rasterizes every frame at 16 pixels per world unit and packs them into this one atlas: a
slime (idle, hop, jump, fall and land), a spinning coin and the sparkle it leaves, a floating
platform with 9-slice ends, tiling ground, sky, stars, mountains and pine hills, the moon, two
clouds, and ground decorations.
