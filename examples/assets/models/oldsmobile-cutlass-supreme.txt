Changes made for this example: the texture of the ground quad under the car - the car's soft
shadow, whose color channels also spelled out the model's title beside the car - was replaced
by the same shadow with the title removed, at 512x512, and the vertex data no material reads
(spare UV sets) was dropped. The geometry was Draco compressed, and the textures were encoded to
KTX2 with full mipmap chains: the normal maps as Basis UASTC (2048x2048 for the body and chrome,
1024x1024 for the wheels and lamps, 512x512 for the license plate) and everything else with
Basis ETC1S supercompression (the 4096x4096 body and chrome maps resampled to 2048x2048, the
license plate to 512x512, the rest at their original size).
