Changes made for this example: the mounted turret and the imported ground plane were
removed, the four wheels and the eight suspension parts were separated onto their own
pivots - each arm onto the lateral axis it really hinges about, at the chassis end of its
run - the body was joined into a single mesh, the model was scaled to 5.2 m long and
turned to face -Z, and the tire and interior materials were made opaque (their alpha
channels were fully opaque already, so blending them only cost depth writes). The geometry
was Draco compressed, and the textures were encoded to KTX2 with Basis ETC1S
supercompression and full mipmap chains: 2048x2048 for the body and tires, 1024x1024 for
the interior and glass.
