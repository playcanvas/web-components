Changes made for this example: the three mesh chunks the conversion had split the surface
into were joined, and the duplicate vertices along their seams were welded - 190,843 down
to 160,801, which is exactly the 401x401 grid the terrain was built on, leaving only the
1,600 edges of its outer rim as boundary. No vertex was moved: the surface is centered by
a translation on the scene root instead, which leaves it 600 m square about the origin
with its lowest point on the ground plane, so the relief is the artist's throughout. The
geometry was Draco compressed, and the 4096x4096 texture was encoded to KTX2 with Basis
ETC1S supercompression and a full mipmap chain.
