Raccoon head from Google's MediaPipe Face Virtual Avatar Demo, by MediaPipe Preview on CodePen:

https://codepen.io/mediapipe-preview/pen/oNPKmEy

The demo loaded the model from https://assets.codepen.io/9177687/raccoon_head.glb, which is
no longer available. The demo's code is Copyright 2023 The MediaPipe Authors, under the Apache
License 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). No separate license is stated for
the model.

Changes made for this example, with glTF-Transform: the ears and hair were simplified to about
half their triangles and the tufts to about a quarter (the head keeps all of its triangles and
its 52 face blendshape morph targets), the two materials were merged into one, the two
4096x4096 JPEG textures were resized to 512x512 PNGs, and the geometry was quantized
(KHR_mesh_quantization) and compressed with meshoptimizer (EXT_meshopt_compression), taking the
file from 9.2 MB to 631 KB.
