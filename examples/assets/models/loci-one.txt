Loci One, a fictional spatial camera, modeled for this repo's Scroll Story example entirely from
code: a Blender 5.2 script builds the body, controls, lens barrel, twelve glass elements and
nine-blade iris with bmesh, keys one 12 second animation clip ("story") that the page scrubs, and
exports this file.

Materials use the glTF extensions the engine renders: KHR_materials_transmission, _volume, _ior,
_dispersion and _iridescence for the glass, _anisotropy for the turned aluminum, _clearcoat for the
enamel, _sheen for the leatherette, _specular and _emissive_strength. Geometry is Draco compressed;
the two tiling normal maps (leatherette grain, brushing) are generated with numpy.

Real size, in meters: the body is 128 x 76 x 38 mm, the lens a 35 mm f/1.4 with its elements stacked
by a small solver so neighbors never touch.
