Scroll Story sounds, generated for this example with ElevenLabs (https://elevenlabs.io):
the ambient bed with ElevenLabs Music (music_v2_5), the camera's mechanical effects with ElevenLabs
Sound Effects (eleven_text_to_sound_v2), then trimmed, normalized and - for the loops - given a
baked-in crossfade by utils/scroll-story/sound.mjs.

- loci-ambience.mp3: ambient bed, loops
- loci-servo.mp3: lens servo whir, loops; its level follows how fast the lens is moving
- loci-blades.mp3: iris blade friction, loops; its level follows the blades
- loci-dial.mp3: a dial detent, played at each stop the dials turn through
- loci-shutter.mp3: the shutter, as the iris closes
- loci-whoosh.mp3: chapter changes
- loci-tick.mp3: the interface
