area-light-luts.json holds the two 64x64 lookup tables of the linearly transformed cosines
technique for real-time area lights:

Eric Heitz, Jonathan Dupuy, Stephen Hill and David Neubelt, "Real-Time Polygonal-Light Shading
with Linearly Transformed Cosines", ACM SIGGRAPH 2016.

https://eheitzresearch.wordpress.com/415-2/

The file is the PlayCanvas Engine's copy of the tables (examples/assets/json/area-light-luts.json
in https://github.com/playcanvas/engine), MIT licensed, in the format `<pc-app area-light-luts>`
expects: `LTC_MAT_1` and `LTC_MAT_2`, 16384 numbers each.
